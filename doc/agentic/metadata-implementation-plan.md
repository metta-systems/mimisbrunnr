# Metadata tables — implementation plan

Design: [metadata-design.md](metadata-design.md) · Decisions: [metadata-decisions.md](metadata-decisions.md) · Order: [operations-plan.md](operations-plan.md)

## Records

- [ ] **METADATA7 — `ObjectRecord` with content locator; one table.** The METADATA-DESIGN§1.1 record with `record_version`, `ContentKind` and the `ExtentLocator` / `PiecesLocator` / `RemoteLocator` / inline variants (METADATA-DESIGN§1.2) replaces today's record and `ObjectLocation`; tag ids, counts and `overflow_offset` go, and the engine reads an object's assertions only from its forward entry; `modified_ns` moves on payload writes only, `state_ns` on state changes, and `ForwardHead.modified_ns` on direct-term changes (INDEX-DESIGN§2.1). Removes `LocationTable`, `location_leaf.rs`, `OverflowRecord` and `BlockKind::OverflowRecord`. Prerequisite: [INDEX14](index-implementation-plan.md). Acceptance: layout and offsets asserted in code and `validation/`; an object with 20 tags and 5 attributes round-trips with its record untouched by tagging (record leaf not dirtied, asserted in the test) while its forward entry's `modified_ns` advances; a 40-byte object is stored and read back `Inline` with no blob allocation or backpointer, and rewriting it with 41 bytes moves it out of the record; a 30-byte object tagged with a tag whose storage policy sets encryption is not stored inline and reads back decrypted from its own storage; tagging an existing inline object with such a tag moves its content out in the same commit (reopen shows no `Inline` record and an encrypted extent or slot); a decoder given an unknown `record_version` fails with a defined `MetaError`.

## Radix trees

- [ ] **METADATA1 — Multi-level radix per origin node.** `ObjectRoots` (METADATA-DESIGN§1.4), descent from a node's entry, inner nodes, COW flush of dirty leaves and ancestors, tree growth, and dropping fully cleared leaves; lifts `MetaError::OidOutOfRange` at `oid_local ≥ 2044`. Prerequisites: METADATA7, [ALLOCATION1](allocation-implementation-plan.md), [BTREE4](btree-implementation-plan.md), [ENGINE3](engine-implementation-plan.md). Acceptance: 5 000 records across the id space of two origin nodes, with equal local ids on both, round-trip without collision; filling leaf 0 then writing a far id grows `root_level`; occupancy bits for ids 5 and 2043 in one leaf survive reload; clearing every record of a leaf drops it at flush, its ids read back as `Cleared`, and the next id allocated after reopen is above the old high-water; on an R = 2 pool each `ObjectRootEntry.root` names two copies and the radix is reachable with either disk missing.
- [ ] **METADATA8 — Content map.** The shared snapshot-aware `BtreeKind::ContentMap` tree keyed `(oid, end_offset, snapshot)` with `PieceEntry` values — private pieces with their replicas, shared pieces by hash (METADATA-DESIGN§1.3); a record moves from `Extent` to `Pieces` on its second piece or first partial overwrite; `AppendPieces`, `ReplacePieces` and `TruncatePieces` replay; overwrites rewrite the pieces they touch and whiteout ancestor-snapshot keys; holes read as zeros; plain pieces capped at one bucket and compressed pieces at `encoded_extent_max`. Prerequisites: METADATA7, [BLOB1](blob-implementation-plan.md), [BTREE4](btree-implementation-plan.md). Acceptance: a 100-piece object round-trips through reopen and reads back byte-exact; a read at offset `x` seeks one key; every piece read verifies against its hash, private or shared; moving one private piece updates only its keys and its backpointer; overwriting 4 KiB in the middle of an object rewrites only the pieces that range touches and reads back byte-exact; after a snapshot, overwriting a range leaves the snapshot's read unchanged and frees no piece the snapshot still names, and deleting the snapshot frees exactly the pieces no key or record version names; a read through an `ExtentLocator` or `PrivatePiece` whose bucket was freed and reused after the write fails with `StaleBlockRef` (METADATA-DESIGN§2.1); a random 4 KiB read of a compressed object decodes at most 128 KiB.

## Wire-form cleanup

- [ ] **METADATA3 — Packed-key backpointer table.** `BackpointerKey` / `BackpointerValue` as plain `repr(C)` persisted through the packed-key codec in `(disk_id, bucket_no, sector_offset)` order; their manual byte-image `Serialize` impls go away; `OwnerKind::PackedBlock` added; `length_sectors` widened to u24 and the `BlobExtent` owner key to `(oid, start_offset)` (METADATA-DESIGN§2.2). Acceptance: a bucket-prefix scan returns exactly that bucket's backpointers; a 256 MiB extent's backpointer round-trips its length.
- [ ] **METADATA4 — Remove CBOR proxies.** Drop `Serialize` / `Deserialize` on `ObjectRecord`, the `ObjectTableKey` / `ObjectTableValue` proxies, and `crates/meta/src/serde_pod_bytes.rs`. Prerequisites: METADATA1, METADATA3, [ALLOCATION6](allocation-implementation-plan.md). Acceptance: no `serialize_bytes(bytemuck::bytes_of` remains in `crates/`.

## Deletion

- [ ] **METADATA6 — Blob reclaim and tombstone expiry.** Deletion keeps the locator until reclaim; reclaim moves the record to `BlobReclaim`, frees what the locator names (extents, extent-list regions, packed slot) or decrements chunk references, and clears the locator in one commit; expiry moves it to `Cleared` and zeroes the rest (METADATA-DESIGN "Object deletion"). On a single node the peer-watermark condition is vacuous and only the grace period applies. Prerequisites: [BLOB1](blob-implementation-plan.md), [ENGINE5](engine-implementation-plan.md), METADATA8, METADATA-D3. Acceptance: delete an object, reopen: it is `Tombstoned` with its extents still allocated; after the grace period reclaim frees them and expiry clears the record; the id is never handed out again.

Backpointer engine wiring: [ENGINE5](engine-implementation-plan.md). Replica population: [BLOB5](blob-implementation-plan.md).
